samples only the photographic panels, excludes the labels and rules, and
normalizes the pale paper when compositing. The original full boards remain
available through “View storyboard”. Approved source logos and V1 are separate
assets; the animation settles to the selected source logo.

Visual direction: a dense drop descends into clear water; fine translucent
filaments and curling membranes unfurl along the two curved lobes; surrounding
ink recedes as the original rain-drop logo becomes clear. Black uses diluted
charcoal; blue uses navy, cobalt and ice blue. No cloud generated from noise,
global spinning or elastic logo deformation.
