# Filing brief review rubric — candidate

Judge the information before judging the prose. No automatic combined score can compensate for a major factual error or hidden material omission.

## Review axes

| Axis | Evidence to examine | Examples of a significant defect |
|---|---|---|
| Factual and scope accuracy | Claim → exact source and qualifiers | Forecast reported as achieved; wrong currency/period; narrower metric inferred from a broader category |
| Material coverage | Source → coverage record → brief | Material commitment or uncertainty omitted; unread notes presented as covered |
| Balance and attribution | Positive statement plus limiting/contrary evidence | Management confidence presented as settled fact; only favorable evidence retained |
| Traceability | User can reach the right source location | Another edition/company cited; quote does not support the assertion; invented page precision |
| Reading utility | Reader can explain business, cash needs and constraints | Promotional paraphrase; generic list of risks without how they affect the business |
| Bilingual consistency | Same item/figure/qualifier in both reports | Translation removes “expected,” changes a unit, or adds a conclusion |

Report material failures individually. For passing items, qualitative strengths and remaining limitations are more useful than a spurious precise rank. Timeliness and cost are separate operational observations.

## Two-way procedure

1. Read the full source inventory independently of the draft. Identify must-understand business facts, qualifications and significant unknowns; this reference set is provisional until adjudicated.
2. Verify the draft's claims, diagrams and numeric transformations against source locations.
3. Inspect the original risk, audit/control, financial-note and material-commitment areas as applicable, plus compressed and pending areas. Test whether an omitted detail would change an included claim.
4. Reconcile the two reads. Record disagreements and source gaps; do not turn reviewer preference into source truth.
5. Review the Chinese/English versions against the same item set.

An omission metric is valid only against a declared reviewed reference set. It does not prove absolute completeness. Report coverage of the actual reference set and residual unread/unavailable source separately.

## Teacher comparison and release

The first comparison is independent production, followed by author-blind human review where feasible. Keep facts tied to original material, never to the teacher answer. Review both producers' shared omissions from raw source.

Separate baseline production from learning: after comparing outputs, record the specific failure and proposed instruction change, then rerun as a new version. Use a held-out company/period to check whether the rule generalizes. Do not feed teacher facts or expected financial values into the general Skill.

Release levels remain explicit: method proposal; candidate Skill; runtime-loaded trial; reviewed report; held-out validation; default method. A proposal or a valid output schema cannot skip the later levels.
